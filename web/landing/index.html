Three hands on every trade.

In the myth, the Moirai spin, measure and cut the thread of fate. Here each sister is a separate part of the system with one job.

  1. Spins

    Clotho

    The council builds the thesis. Six agents read a deterministic quantitative core and argue until a fixed-rule Manager decides.

  2. Measures

    Lachesis

    Risk sizes the order. It is fail-closed, enforces hard ceilings and signs every order it allows with Ed25519.

  3. Cuts

    Atropos

    Execution cuts. It verifies every signature, keeps its own limits and always places a stop with the position.

From raw tick to scored forecast.

Eight steps in one direction, then a loop back: every forecast is scored 12 hours later and the council learns from it.

  1. 01

    Recorder

    Captures CoinMarketCap signals and Binance public market data. Binance is the execution layer and the source of truth.

  2. 02

    Data lake

    Every capture lands in an immutable lake linked by a hash chain. Queries are point-in-time, so no step sees the future.

  3. 03

    Quant core

    Deterministic features, per-agent probabilities and shared price levels. A scanner turns them into trade candidates.

  4. 04

    Council

    Six agents answer blind, then debate for at most three rounds with anonymous claims that code verifies.

  5. 05

    Manager

    A fixed-rule Manager aggregates the council and decides. The last word belongs to rules, not to a model.

  6. 06

    Risk

    Fail-closed. Sizes the order, enforces hard ceilings and signs it with Ed25519. No signature, no order.

  7. 07

    Execution

    The only service holding exchange keys. Verifies the signature, keeps its own limits, always places a STOP and reconciles.

  8. 08

    Scorer

    Labels every forecast 12 hours after it was made. Agent weights learn from the outcomes and flow back to the council.

Six voices, one fixed rule.

Round one is blind: no agent sees another. Then up to three debate rounds, where claims are anonymous and checked by code before they count.

Built to say no.

Every layer between a thesis and the exchange can stop a trade on its own.

Gate G4 before real money

Every strategy trades on paper first and has to clear a statistical shadow gate.

  • ≥ 150independent entry orders
  • passcalibration checks
  • > 1.5annualized daily Sharpe, with PSR(0) ≥ 0.95
  • < 10 %maximum drawdown

Then 1/4 size, on a limited-capital sub-account with a trade-only key.

Fail-closed veto

If Risk cannot approve an order, the order does not exist. A failure anywhere in Risk means no trade.

Signed orders

Risk signs every order with Ed25519. Execution checks the signature before it acts.

Keys in one place

Execution is the only component that holds exchange keys.

Hard ceilings

Risk enforces hard limits on every order. Execution keeps limits of its own.

Kill switch

Execution can stop all trading at once and reconciles what is open.

Paper first

Moirai starts on paper. Real money stays gated by Gate G4, and then starts at a quarter of the size.
